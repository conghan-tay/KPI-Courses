# Arun Velasco — CV

Backend and payments. Bangkok, open to remote in UTC+5 to UTC+9.

## Halberd Systems — Graduate Engineer — Mar 2012 to Nov 2014

Java, Oracle, an internal reporting stack for a company that leased construction
equipment. I wrote batch jobs that produced PDFs nobody read. Two useful things came out
of it: I learned to read a query plan, and I learned that "the report is wrong" is almost
never a report problem.

Left because the team was being wound down and I was told so eight months in advance,
which I have since learned is unusually decent behaviour.

## Speedwell Logistics — Backend Engineer — Jan 2015 to Feb 2016

Route planning and driver dispatch. Python, Postgres, a message queue held together by a
cron job. First time I owned a service that woke me up. First time I put a queue in
Postgres instead of asking for Kafka, which is a habit I have never lost.

## Career gap — Feb 2016 to Jan 2017

Eleven months, not working. My father had a stroke in early 2016 and I moved back to
Penang to be the person who dealt with hospitals and insurers. Nothing about this is
interesting to a hiring manager and I am not going to dress it up as a sabbatical. I did
not build a side project. I read a lot and I was tired.

I mention it here because it is on my CV in plain text and I would rather you read it
here than find it and wonder.

## Rialto Payments — Senior Backend Engineer — Feb 2017 to Aug 2019

My first payments job. Card acquiring for small merchants: authorisation, capture,
refunds, chargeback representment. Two and a half years.

Also where I did the only machine learning I have ever shipped — a gradient-boosted
fraud-scoring model on transaction features, retrained weekly. It worked, in the sense
that it beat the rules engine it replaced. It stopped being my problem in August 2019 and
I have not touched the field since.

## Agoda — Senior, then Staff Engineer — Sep 2019 to Apr 2025

Five and a half years on the supplier payouts platform. Paying hotels, not taking money
from travellers — the other direction, and a genuinely different problem.

Owned, end to end:

- Virtual card issuance for supplier payments
- PSP routing and failover across three providers
- Nightly reconciliation between our ledger, the issuer and the bank
- The payout queue and its workers

Details are in the system write-ups. This is a CV; those are the actual answer.

## Independent — May 2025 to present

Contract backend work, and an unpaid advisory seat at NodusArt. I am specific about what
that second one is and is not — see the NodusArt note.
