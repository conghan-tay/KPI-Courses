Pink is a surface, never an ink.

The reorder moment

After the opening diagnostic, rows translate to their new position in 160ms steps(4), staggered 60ms. Skipped lessons take the hatch treatment and drop to faint ink. This is the entire argument against video — it has to be seen.

Why blocks, not a bar

One cell per lesson, filled in a single steps(1) snap. A snap reads as an achievement; a smooth fill reads as loading.

§7 · the fast ways to break this
  • ✕ Pink text on white. Ever. (1.92:1)
  • ✕ Blurred shadows, gradients, glass, or glow.
  • ✕ Any container radius between 1px and 998px.
  • ✕ A third hue — not for charts, not for one badge.
  • ✕ Uppercase runs over eight words.
  • ✕ Symmetric chat bubbles.
  • ✕ CSS-blurring locked content instead of withholding it.
  • ✕ Easing curves. Motion here is stepped.
  • ✕ Spinners and shimmer skeletons — use halftone.
  • ✕ Softening the chrome to make /learn comfortable. Fix the text column instead.
